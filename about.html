About Pinviddl

Pinviddl helps you check what media is available from a public Pinterest Pin. Paste one Pin link, preview files the source makes available, and save a supported file in your browser.

Open the downloader Read our Privacy Policy

A downloader with clear limits

Here is what Pinviddl can do, and what depends on the Pin itself.

01

One public Pin at a time

Use a link to one publicly accessible Pin. A pin.it link works when it resolves to one Pin; board, profile, and search links are not supported.

02

The source sets the format

Available videos, images, and animations depend on the files exposed by the Pin. Pinviddl previews supported files and does not convert formats or create missing media.

03

Public does not mean reusable

A public Pin may still be protected by copyright. Save or share media only when you own it or have permission to use it.

How it works

From Pin link to available file

Paste a link to one public Pin into the downloader. Pinviddl checks the link, looks for media it can access, and shows the available files so you can choose what to save.

A Pin may expose an MP4 or another video file, an image, an animated GIF, or only a preview. The format and quality come from the source, so results can differ from one Pin to another.

Privacy, in plain language

You do not need a Pinterest login or a Pinviddl account. The submitted link is processed to look up the public Pin; Pinviddl does not keep a download-history database. Some network data may be processed temporarily for rate limiting and security.
