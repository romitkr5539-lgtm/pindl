Tips for choosing a Pinterest download

Start with the individual Pin

Open the specific Pin you want and copy its link from Pinterest. A full Pin URL or a pin.it share link can work when it leads to one public Pin. Board, profile, and search links do not identify a single media item.

Check the preview and file details

Preview the media before saving so you can confirm it is the right image or clip. If more than one image size is available, compare the listed dimensions; for animation, check whether the file is a GIF or MP4. Pinviddl saves the available source file without enlarging or converting it.

Make sure you have permission to use it

Being able to view a public Pin does not automatically grant permission to repost or use its image or video. Save your own content or material you are authorized to use. See Pinterest’s copyright guidance for more information.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about supported Pins, available file types, privacy, and downloads.

How do I download a Pinterest video?

Copy the link to one public video Pin, including a pin.it link, and paste it into Pinviddl. Select Download, review the available media, and choose the file to save.

Can I download a Pinterest video as MP4?

Pinviddl can provide an MP4 when a public Pin exposes a directly downloadable video file. Not every Pin has one, and quality depends on the source. Pinterest’s built-in download option does not download video Pins. Read Pinterest’s download guidance.

How do I download a Pinterest image?

Copy the URL of one public image Pin, paste it into Pinviddl, and select Download. Available image sizes depend on what Pinterest exposes; Pinviddl does not upscale images.

Can I download a Pinterest GIF?

Pinviddl can provide a GIF when the public Pin exposes one. Some animated Pins are served as MP4 video instead. Pinviddl offers the available file and does not convert formats.

Does Pinviddl work with pin.it links?

Yes. Paste a pin.it link if it opens one public Pin. Full Pinterest Pin URLs also work. Links to boards, profiles, search pages, or the home feed are not supported.

Is Pinviddl free, and do I need a Pinterest account?

Pinviddl is free to use in your browser. You do not need to sign in to Pinterest, create a Pinviddl account, or install an extension.

Why isn’t my Pinterest link working?

The Pin may be private, deleted, or restricted, or it may not expose a direct media file. Pinviddl cannot access private Pins or turn an adaptive stream into a downloadable file.

Where are my Pinterest downloads saved?

Your browser saves files to its usual Downloads folder or asks where to save them. On iPhone or iPad, check Safari Downloads or the Downloads folder in Files.

Can I reuse a Pinterest image or video I download?

A public Pin is not automatically free to repost or reuse. Download content you own or have permission to use, and check with the copyright holder when needed. See Pinterest’s copyright guidance.

Does Pinviddl work on phones?

Yes. Pinviddl works in mobile browsers, including Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. You can paste a Pin URL or a pin.it link copied from Pinterest.
