How Pinviddl handles a video Pin

The downloader checks one public Pin link and lists direct video files it can access. The Pin determines which formats and quality are available.

Use a link to one Pin

Paste a full Pinterest Pin URL or a pin.it share link that opens one public Pin. Links to boards, profiles, and search results do not identify a single video.

Save the available format

A Pin may expose an MP4, MOV, or WebM file. Pinviddl shows file details when available and does not convert or re-encode the video.

Know when a file is unavailable

Private, deleted, restricted, or stream-only Pins may not expose a downloadable video file. Pinviddl does not bypass access controls.

Using the video downloader on your device

On a phone or tablet

Open Pinviddl in your browser, copy a public video's Pin link from Pinterest, paste it into the box, and choose Download. If your browser asks, confirm where to save the file. On iPhone or Android, check the browser's downloads list or the device's Files app.

On a computer

Paste the individual Pin URL or supported pin.it link, review the preview and format, then download the file. Most browsers show it in the Downloads list; the saved location depends on your browser settings.

If no video file appears

First check that your link opens one public Pin rather than a board or profile. A Pin can play on Pinterest yet expose only a segmented stream instead of a direct file. Pinviddl does not combine or convert those streams, so a downloadable video may not be available for every public Pin.

Privacy and reuse

Pinviddl processes the submitted link to retrieve public media and does not keep a download history. Read the Privacy Policy for details. A public video is not automatically free to repost; save content you own or have permission to use. See Pinterest’s copyright guidance.

Frequently asked questions

Answers about available formats, Pin links, mobile downloads, and common problems.

How do I download a Pinterest video?

Copy the link to one public video Pin, paste it into Pinviddl, and choose Download. Preview the available video and select a file to save it in your browser.

What video formats can Pinviddl download?

Pinviddl can save direct MP4, MOV, or WebM files when they are available from a public Pin. The format depends on the source, and Pinviddl does not convert video files.

Does a Pinterest video download include sound?

If the video file provided by the Pin includes an audio track, the downloaded file keeps it. Pinviddl does not re-encode video files.

Does Pinviddl work with pin.it links?

Yes. Paste a pin.it link if it opens one public Pinterest Pin. Links to boards, profiles, and search results are not supported.

Why can’t I download a Pinterest video?

Check that the link opens one public Pin. The Pin may be private, deleted, or restricted, or it may not expose a direct video file that Pinviddl can download.

Can I download a video from a private Pinterest board?

No. Pinviddl works with publicly accessible Pins and does not bypass private boards or sign-in restrictions.

Where is my Pinterest video saved on my phone?

Your browser usually lists the download in its Downloads section. On a phone, check the browser’s download list or the device’s Files app; the exact location depends on your settings.

Can I repost a Pinterest video after downloading it?

A public Pin is not automatically free to repost or reuse. Download videos you own or have permission to use, and check with the copyright holder when needed. See Pinterest’s copyright guidance.

Is Pinviddl free, and do I need a Pinterest account?

Yes. Pinviddl is free to use, and you do not need a Pinterest sign-in or Pinviddl account. The tool can only check Pins that are publicly accessible.
