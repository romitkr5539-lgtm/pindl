Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Pin requestsEmail inquiriesCookies and storageThird partiesRetentionContact

This policy explains the information processed when you use Pinviddl or contact us. Pinviddl does not require an account or your Pinterest login to check a public Pin.

1. When you check a Pin

When you submit a Pin URL, Pinviddl processes the address to resolve the public Pin and look up media that can be made available to your browser. The request may include your IP address and standard connection information.

  • Pinviddl does not maintain a database of submitted Pin URLs or download history.
  • IP addresses may be processed temporarily in memory for rate limiting (typically about 60 seconds) and through Cloudflare’s standard edge security telemetry.
  • Public Pin metadata may be briefly cached at the edge (for example, for up to five minutes) to help serve requests.
  • Pinviddl does not keep permanent copies of downloaded media in a user library.

2. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive the message and use its contents to respond and handle the inquiry. Your email provider may also process or store that message under its own terms.

Please do not include your Pinterest password or other sensitive information in a support message.

3. Cookies and browser storage

Pinviddl does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or fingerprinting scripts. Cloudflare may use temporary security measures, including protection cookies, to help maintain network security and availability.

4. Services and links from other companies

Pinviddl relies on Cloudflare infrastructure to deliver the site and process requests. Pin lookups and media requests involve Pinterest services and its media hosts. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. If you choose to email us, your email application and provider handle that message. Those services have their own privacy practices.

5. Retention and your choices

Pinviddl does not keep a visitor account or download-history database. Temporary request processing, security telemetry, brief media-metadata caching, and any email you choose to send are handled as described above. You can choose not to submit a Pin link or contact us; if you have a privacy question about an email you sent, use the Contact page.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, visit the Contact page. The rights available to you can depend on where you live and the circumstances of your request.
